Privacy Policy

This policy explains how noisonnoiton (the “blog”) handles visitor information and cookies.

Scope

Section titled “Scope”

This policy applies to the blog’s public pages and third-party services embedded in the blog. Websites and services reached through external links follow their own policies.

Information handled directly by the blog

Section titled “Information handled directly by the blog”

The blog does not operate user registration or a self-hosted comment system. Information provided when a visitor writes a Giscus comment with a GitHub account or contacts the blog through GitHub may be handled according to those services and the visitor’s public-profile settings.

Cookies, advertising, and analytics tools

Section titled “Cookies, advertising, and analytics tools”

The blog may include the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense: Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads. Google’s advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to show personalized ads based on visits to this blog or other websites.
  • Google Analytics 4: Used to understand aggregated visitor statistics and site usage flows.
  • Kakao AdFit: May be used to serve advertising.
  • Giscus: A GitHub Discussions-based comment feature. Writing a comment uses a GitHub account and is subject to GitHub’s policies.

Advertising availability and the set of ad partners may change as the blog evolves.

Opting out of personalized advertising

Section titled “Opting out of personalized advertising”

You can manage or opt out of Google personalized advertising in Google’s Ad Center. You can opt out of personalized advertising from some other third-party vendors at About Ads Choices.
